# Pixel Pin comes to the Mac

Pixel Pin is now available for Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later.

- Open the DMG and drag Pixel Pin into Applications. The installer includes guidance in Korean, English, and Simplified Chinese.
- After you grant access, sampling follows your pointer across displays. Prefer the system screen picker? You can choose it in Settings.
- Copy HEX, RGB, HSL, HSV, or reference CMYK values, with a magnifier to find the right pixel.
- Choose light, dark, or system appearance, switch between three languages, and keep your recent colors close.
- Open Settings with `⌘ + ,`. Click outside or press Esc to dismiss it. The close button minimizes to the Dock by default; Settings lets you change it to quit.
- Install in Applications to enable Sparkle updates and the Check for Updates command.

This release is not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. macOS may show a security warning or ask you to renew Screen Recording access. Sparkle update signatures authenticate updates; they are separate from Apple notarization.

Screen colors use sRGB and do not preserve the full brightness or gamut of HDR. CMYK values are estimates without a print profile.
