Pixel Pin v1.1.1 adds local data controls, clearer policies, and accessibility improvements.

## App

- **Clear recent colors** removes current color history and any legacy Digital Color Meter history.
- **Restore default preferences** resets appearance, language, and color format. Recent colors and Windows clipboard history are retained.
- Open the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Support & payments pages in the app’s language from Settings.
- Improved secondary text contrast in light mode and keyboard focus indicators.

## Website

- Terms, privacy, and support/payment information are available in Korean, English, and Simplified Chinese.
- Every page includes policy links and a website preference reset control.
- Improved text contrast and refreshed the settings examples.

## Before you download

- Windows 10 / 11, x64. Unzip and run `PixelPin.exe`. The .NET runtime is included.
- The app is free. Optional Lemon Squeezy purchases are under consideration; no checkout is active.
- Screen images and recent colors are not uploaded to TeamHJD servers. App data stays on this PC. The Privacy Policy separately explains GitHub Pages access logging.
- Builds remain unsigned, so Windows may display a protection warning. Check the official source and SHA-256 file.
- HDR output is converted to sRGB. CMYK is a reference conversion without an ICC print profile.

Validation covered 15 website routes, localized policy navigation and preference reset, text contrast, and app history deletion and error handling. No known vulnerable package was reported at the time of the check.
